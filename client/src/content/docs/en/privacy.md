## Diagnostic Data

We process your exit IP, browser environment metadata, and network leak test signals to generate your report, and retain diagnostic telemetry records to debug false positives and improve accuracy. Canvas rendering output is submitted only as an irreversible local hash (raw pixels are never uploaded); Audio checks inspect only whether native methods appear overridden, and WebGL submits the GPU renderer string solely to check OS consistency.

The dedicated DNS, WebRTC, and IPv6 leak test pages store the last two successful runs locally in your browser (IndexedDB) so you can compare results before and after configuration changes.

## Feedback

When you submit optional feedback, we store the selected category, your note, and the associated scan record for troubleshooting. An optional reply email is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

## Shareable Images & Text

Shareable PNG cards and text summaries are generated locally inside your browser without being uploaded to our servers, and full IP addresses are masked automatically.

## Privacy Requests

We do not sell or trade your personal data.

To request access to or deletion of associated records, contact [privacy@detectradar.com](mailto:privacy@detectradar.com).
